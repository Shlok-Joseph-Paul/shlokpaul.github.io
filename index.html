Selected research

Four connected directions

My work moves between materials chemistry, device design, scalable fabrication, and the tools needed to study all three.

01

Photodetectors

Heavy-metal-free Ag₂Se quantum-dot photodiodes

Solution-phase, ligand-exchanged Ag₂Se inks integrated into fast, sensitive near- and short-wave infrared photodiodes.

02

Scalable fabrication

Biphasic dip-coating for CQD thin films

Automated layer-by-layer coating that reduces material waste while enabling uniform large-area and non-planar films.

03

Device architecture

Transparent electrodes for top-illuminated devices

Ag-nanowire/polymer electrodes for flexible and top-illuminated infrared photodetectors.

04

Current interests

Carrier dynamics, gain, and AI-for-science

Transient optical and electrical measurements, high-throughput experimentation, and data-driven materials optimization.

Tools & systems

Building the infrastructure for better experiments.

I develop experimental tools and analysis workflows that make fabrication and characterization more reproducible, scalable, and data-rich.

01

Automated three-stage dip-coater

Motorized adsorption, ligand exchange, and rinsing workflow for layer-by-layer CQD film fabrication.

02

Photodetector characterization workflows

IV, responsivity, noise, transient response, and frequency-response measurements using lock-in amplifiers, SMUs, and current preamplifiers.

03

Data analysis and visualization

Python and Origin-based workflows for extracting device metrics, plotting datasets, and preparing publication-quality figures.
